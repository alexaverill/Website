Leatherman Screwdriver

I recently purchased a leatherman ratcheting bit driver to add to my toolkit that I take with me when I go onsite for exhibit installs at museums. The full list of what I try to keep in my toolkit.

Usually however I end up taking a leatherman, and then only using it as a pair of pliers and a screwdriver. I started by printing an existing adaptor to make the racheting driver usable on its own, but that ended up being too small to be comfortable.

Since I have a 3d printer I decided to just go ahead and make my own that turned it into a more full sized screwdriver. The final model is on Printables. It supports the both the bit extender and ratcheting bit holder.
